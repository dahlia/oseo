this task's replacement rule.

deletable eval-local bindings or the selected caller/environment matrix.

witness construction of eval environments; no emulation was added.

ADR 0016 amendment admitting the relevant eval form, or evidence of an
uncovered non-source subject under ADR 0019. The architectural laboratory,
staged-compilation and WinterTC triggers remain in force.
